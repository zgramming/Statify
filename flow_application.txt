1. buat survey
2. hit endpoint ->  survey response -> pending 
3. kirim value-nya
4. hitn endpoint -> sent survey kalo berhasil, kalo gagal -> fail survey



-------------------
flow pas user terima sms
1. ketika sms masuk, hit endpoint machines/:machineId/surveys/:number, terus check apakah kosong/ngga.
1.1. kalo kosong dan kontentnya "ok", hit enpoint machines/:machineId/surveys (create)
1.2 ketika success, check lock apakah true/false
1.2.1. ketika true, hit endpoint surveys/:surveyId/unlock (unlock survey)
1.2.2. ketika false, hit endpoint {{url}}/surveys/:surveyId/responses (create survey response)

flow kirim sms
1. hit endpoint {{url}}/machines/:machineId/pending-response (pending response)
2. Check apakah pending response ada isinya atau ngga
2.1. Kalau Ada isinya, kirim sms (native) ke nomor yang ada di obj survey, dengan value yang ada,
lalu check apakah ketika kirim sms berhasil / gagal
2.1.1. kalau berhasil, hint endpoint {{url}}/survey-responses/:surveyResponseId/sent (sent survey response)
2.1.2 kalau gagal, hit endpoint  {{url}}/survey-responses/:surveyResponseId/fail (fail sent survey)

2.2. Kalau ngga ada isinya, hit endpoint {{url}}/machines/:machineId/surveys/:surveyId (create survey)
